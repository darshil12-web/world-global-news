Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to GameWorld. We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit and play games on our platform.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account to play our games. However, we automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information, including:

  • Device and browser information (e.g., Chrome, iOS, Android).
  • Usage data, such as which games you play and how long you play them.
  • IP addresses for security and regional localization.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like most modern websites, GameWorld uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These cookies help us save your game progress locally on your device, analyze traffic patterns, and deliver relevant advertisements.

4. Third-Party Advertising

We partner with third-party ad networks to display advertisements. These partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet.

5. Children's Privacy

GameWorld is designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will promptly remove it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
